Shelf · Book log for Android

Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026.

Shelf is a book log. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your library lives on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Every book you add, with its cover, rating and notes, is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Nobody else can see it, including the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

If Android's own device backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's storage in that backup, as it does for most apps. That is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

What leaves your phone

When you add a book, Shelf looks it up so it can fill in the cover and ISBN for you:

  • The title and author you type are sent to Google Books and Open Library as a search.
  • The cover image of the book you pick is downloaded from the same service.

Nothing else is sent: not your other books, ratings, notes, or anything identifying you beyond what any internet request carries (such as your IP address). Those services handle the request under their own policies: Google's privacy policy and the Internet Archive's terms (which runs Open Library). All requests use encrypted HTTPS.

Photos you add

If you choose your own cover photo, Shelf asks Android to let you pick one image. Only that image is used, and it is stored with the book on your phone. Shelf cannot see your other photos.

Backups you make

"Back up" in settings writes one file with your whole library to a place you choose. It goes nowhere unless you move or share it yourself. "Restore" reads a backup file you pick.

Children

Shelf is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
